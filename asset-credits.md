## 2. Archival Visual Assets & Plates
All visual plates have been generated using curated historical documentary parameters reflecting authentic silver gelatin, 35mm film grain, letterpress typography, and museum conservation documentation standards.

### Plate 01: Hero Master Portrait (ACC.1960.01.01)
- **Subject:** Elder Nigerian scholar and artisan in hand-woven adire attire, contemplative natural window light.
- **Reference Style:** Mid-20th century Nigerian documentary studio photography (Leica 35mm, silver gelatin print).
- **Physical Path:** `/src/assets/images/hero_archival_portrait_1792138903067.jpg`
- **Curatorial Annotation:** Evokes the dignified intellectual atmosphere of postcolonial self-determination.

### Plate 02: Featured Story — The Zaria Rebels (ACC.1958.09.14)
- **Subject:** Nigerian College of Arts, Science and Technology art studio in Zaria, October 1958.
- **Reference Style:** Documentary silver gelatin contact sheet; artists reviewing Uli-inspired linocuts and modern easel paintings.
- **Physical Path:** `/src/assets/images/featured_story_zaria_1792138904029.jpg`
- **Curatorial Annotation:** Represents the historical moment of "Natural Synthesis" formulated by Uche Okeke, Demas Nwoko, and Bruce Onobrakpeya.

### Plate 03: The Sound Archive — Lagos Studio Session (ACC.1960.10.01)
- **Subject:** Highlife brass section, tenor saxophone, and talking drum (gangan) player around a vintage ribbon microphone.
- **Reference Style:** 1950s/60s Decca West Africa & Philips recording session photography.
- **Physical Path:** `/src/assets/images/sound_archive_session_1792138904421.jpg`
- **Curatorial Annotation:** Captures the acoustic synthesis of jazz brass and Yoruba sacred percussion.

### Plate 04: The Style Archive — Indigo Resist & Aso-Oke Weaving (ACC.1934.04.12)
- **Subject:** Hand-dyed Adire Eleko cassava starch resist cloth and antique strip-woven Aso-Oke textile displayed on archival conservation linen with accession markers.
- **Reference Style:** Museum conservation photographic record.
- **Physical Path:** `/src/assets/images/style_archive_textile_1792138906120.jpg`
- **Curatorial Annotation:** Preserves the sacred geometric patterns (*Olokun*, *Ibadandun*) created by master dyers (*Iya Alaro*) of Abeokuta.

### Plate 05: The Printed Archive — The West African Pilot (ACC.1944.11.18)
- **Subject:** Weathered 1944 broadsheet newspaper layout with woodblock lead typography and Dr. Nnamdi Azikiwe speech headline.
- **Reference Style:** Letterpress print on aged wood-pulp newsprint.
- **Physical Path:** `/src/assets/images/print_archive_press_1792138905450.jpg`
- **Curatorial Annotation:** Documents anticolonial print culture and the typographic power of nationalist journalism.

## 3. Historical & Scholarly Citations
The narratives, quotations, and cultural facts presented in ARCHIVE were researched from the following institutions and scholarly references:

1. **The Zaria Art Society & Natural Synthesis:**
   - Okeke-Agulu, Chika. *Postcolonial Modernism: Art and Decolonization in Twentieth-Century Nigeria*. Duke University Press, 2015.
   - Uche Okeke, *Natural Synthesis Manifesto*, Zaria, October 1958.
   - Asele Institute Archives, Nimo, Anambra State.

2. **The Golden Age of Lagos Highlife:**
   - Collins, John. *Highlife Time: The History of West African Popular Music*. Dak’Art Publications, 1994.
   - Waterman, Christopher Alan. *Jùjú: A Social History and Ethnography of an African Popular Music*. University of Chicago Press, 1990.
   - Decca West Africa & Philips Phonographic Catalogues (Lagos Studio Logs, 1952–1966).

3. **Adire Eleko & Yoruba Textile Arts:**
   - Wolff, Norma H. & Wahab, O. *Adire Cloth in Nigeria: The Visual and Social Language of Indigo*. University of Washington Press.
   - Egba Traditional Crafts Guild Documentation, Abeokuta Heritage Centre.

4. **The West African Pilot & Nigerian Nationalist Press:**
   - Azikiwe, Nnamdi. *My Odyssey: An Autobiography*. C. Hurst & Co., 1970.
   - National Archives of Nigeria (Ibadan Branch), Newspaper Collection Record Group PR/PILOT/1937–1960.

5. **Igun Eronmwon & Benin Metallurgy:**
   - Ben-Amos, Paula Girshick. *The Art of Benin*. British Museum Press, 1995.
   - Royal Court of Benin / Guild of Royal Bronze Casters Chronicles, Igun Street, Benin City.

6. **The Mbari Artists and Writers Club:**
   - Beier, Ulli. *In a Hotel Garden: The Mbari Club of Ibadan*. Bayreuth African Studies, 1991.
   - Special Collections, Kenneth Dike Library, University of Ibadan.

7. **J.D. 'Okhai Ojeikere Photography:**
   - Magnin, André (ed.). *J.D. ’Okhai Ojeikere: Photographs*. Fondation Cartier pour l’art contemporain / Scalo, 2000.

8. **Lemi Ghariokwu Afrobeat Graphics:**
   - Veal, Michael E. *Fela: The Life & Times of an African Musical Icon*. Temple University Press, 2000.
   - Lemi Ghariokwu Studio Archives, Lagos.

9. **Hausa Tubali Architecture:**
   - Dmochowski, Z.R. *An Introduction to Nigerian Traditional Architecture, Vol. 1: Northern Nigeria*. Ethnographica / National Commission for Museums and Monuments, 1990.

## 4. Audio Engine & Soundscape
- **Technology:** Real-time synthesis using the native Web Audio API (`AudioContext`).
- **Composition:**
  - Archival 1950s/60s shellac vinyl surface texture (bandpass-filtered noise + stochastic micro-crackle pulses).
  - Ambient meditative pentatonic resonance on traditional West African intervals (G3, A3, C4, D4, E4).
- **Licensing:** Synthesized entirely client-side without external proprietary audio files.
